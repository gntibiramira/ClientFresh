About Fresh Drop Rwanda

Your Trusted Grocery Partner in Kigali

Fresh Drop Rwanda is a premier grocery delivery service committed to bringing the freshest and highest quality products directly to your doorstep in Kigali.

Founded with a passion for quality and convenience, we source our products from trusted local farmers and suppliers, ensuring that you receive the best quality meat, fruits, vegetables, drinks, and organic foods.

Our Mission

To make grocery shopping convenient, reliable, and enjoyable while supporting local producers and promoting healthy eating habits in our community.

Our Values

  • Quality First: We never compromise on the freshness and quality of our products
  • Customer Focus: Your satisfaction is our top priority
  • Local Support: We partner with local farmers and producers
  • Sustainability: We promote eco-friendly practices in our operations
